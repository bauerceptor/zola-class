## Notes

### Opening (2 min)

- Greet the audience, state the problem in one sentence
- The hook: why this matters right now

### Background (5 min)

Walk through the related work briskly. The audience knows the
field — you do not need to explain everything, just anchor
your contribution relative to the state of the art.

### Main contribution (15 min)

This is the core. Slow down here. Repeat the key insight twice —
once before the details, once after.

### Results (5 min)

Two or three numbers. Do not read every cell of the table.
Highlight the one comparison that proves the point.

### Conclusion (3 min)

Restate the problem, restate the solution, say what comes next.

## Anticipated questions

**Q: Why not use method X instead?**
A: Method X fails at scale because Y. We show this in Table 2.

**Q: How sensitive is this to the hyperparameter Z?**
A: Ablation in the appendix — performance degrades gracefully
   but the optimal range is narrow, which is a limitation.
